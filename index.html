MTB CATEGORY

DOWNHILL

BUILT TO DESCEND.

Downhill bikes are designed around descending steep, technical terrain at high speed. They prioritise suspension travel, stability, control and confidence when the trail gets rough.

LONG-TRAVEL SUSPENSION Designed to handle large impacts and rough terrain.
STABILITY Geometry is focused on keeping the bike controlled at speed.
POWERFUL BRAKING Strong braking performance is important for steep descents.
BIKE PARKS & DH Ideal for dedicated downhill trails and bike parks.

WHO IS IT FOR?

Downhill bikes are best suited to riders who spend a lot of time descending and want maximum control and suspension performance. They are less focused on efficient climbing.

MTB CATEGORY

ENDURO

CLIMB UP.
SEND IT DOWN.

Enduro bikes are built to handle challenging descents while still being capable of getting you back to the top. They are a strong choice for riders who want a serious all-round performance bike.

DESCENT PERFORMANCE Built for technical and aggressive descents.
CLIMBING ABILITY More efficient uphill than a dedicated downhill bike.
VERSATILITY Great for local trails, mountains and bike parks.
LONG-TRAVEL SETUPS Typically designed with substantial suspension travel.

WHO IS IT FOR?

Enduro is a great option for riders who want to ride challenging terrain without giving up the ability to pedal the bike uphill.

MTB CATEGORY

TRAIL

THE ALL-ROUNDER.

Trail bikes are designed to cover a broad range of riding. They balance climbing efficiency with enough suspension and control for technical descents.

VERSATILE Suitable for many different types of trail riding.
CLIMBING Generally more efficient uphill than longer-travel bikes.
DESCENDING Capable of handling technical trails while staying playful.
EVERYDAY RIDING A strong choice if you want one bike for lots of different rides.

WHO IS IT FOR?

Trail bikes are ideal for riders who want one versatile bike that can handle normal local rides as well as more demanding trails.
