MTB TYPE

DOWNHILL

Pure gravity. Maximum speed. Maximum control. Downhill bikes are designed specifically for descending steep, rough and technical terrain.

WHAT IS A DOWNHILL BIKE?

A downhill mountain bike is designed primarily for going downhill as fast and confidently as possible. They normally have long-travel suspension, aggressive geometry and powerful braking systems.

They are ideal if your main focus is bike parks, downhill racing, steep technical trails and jumps.

IDEAL FOR

Bike parks, downhill tracks, racing, steep descents, jumps and very technical terrain.

NOT IDEAL FOR

Long-distance climbing or riders looking for one bike to do everything.

MTB TYPE

ENDURO

The ultimate balance between climbing ability and aggressive downhill performance.

WHAT IS AN ENDURO BIKE?

Enduro bikes are designed to handle demanding descents while still being capable of climbing back to the top.

They are a great choice for riders who want one mountain bike capable of handling aggressive trails, alpine riding, bike parks and everyday trail rides.

IDEAL FOR

Aggressive trail riding, Alps trips, bike parks, technical descents and mixed terrain.

NOT IDEAL FOR

Riders who mainly want lightweight XC-style riding or pure downhill racing.

MTB TYPE

TRAIL

Versatile, fun and capable. Trail bikes are designed to handle a huge variety of mountain terrain.

WHAT IS A TRAIL BIKE?

Trail bikes sit between lightweight cross-country bikes and more aggressive enduro bikes.

They are excellent for riders who want a bike that feels fun and efficient on climbs while still being capable on technical descents.

IDEAL FOR

Local trails, all-round riding, long rides, technical trails and riders who want versatility.

NOT IDEAL FOR

Pure downhill racing or extremely aggressive enduro riding.
